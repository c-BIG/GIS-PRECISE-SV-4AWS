# Citation

This project is an AWS HealthOmics port of the Broad Institute's
[GATK-SV](https://github.com/broadinstitute/gatk-sv) pipeline. If you use this software,
please cite the gnomAD-SV paper that describes the underlying method, as requested by the
GATK-SV team:

> Collins, R.L., Brand, H., Karczewski, K.J. et al.
> **A structural variation reference for medical and population genetics.**
> *Nature* 581, 444–451 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2287-8

Upstream pipeline and its documentation:
- GATK-SV: https://github.com/broadinstitute/gatk-sv
- Documentation: https://broadinstitute.github.io/gatk-sv/docs/intro
- Additional references: https://broadinstitute.github.io/gatk-sv/docs/references/
